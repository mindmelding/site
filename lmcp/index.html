How it works

↓
  1. 01

    Your agent reads your dining spend

    Muse or Instinct pulls the restaurant, bar and coffee lines it already sees from Plaid or Monarch: the merchant name, amount and date, and nothing more.

  2. 02

    Loyalty MCP matches the real location

    Each card line is matched to one restaurant by slug and phone number, never by "a similar name in the same city." Then it checks whether that location's program is actually on.

  3. 03

    You get one clear report

    You see what you missed, which places are worth joining first, and a direct signup link for each. Places with no program, and places it couldn't confirm, are listed separately.

The report you get back

It's short enough to read in your chat app: the bottom line first, then the best place to join, the full breakdown, and the fine print.

Your agent prints it exactly as returned, without rewording it.

Real output from ten sample transactions in North County San Diego.

What it won't do

↓

It never signs you up

You get the signup link and decide for yourself. It has no way to enroll you, so it can't do it by accident.

It never sends your personal info

It only sees merchant names, amounts and dates. Your phone, email and account numbers never leave your agent, and request contents aren't logged.

It doesn't guess

If it can't confirm a program, it says "couldn't tell." It never reports "no rewards" without checking, and it never gives one location credit for another's program.

It doesn't promise back pay

The "missed" amount shows what joining is worth. Rewards programs don't pay out retroactively, and the report says so.
